Questions

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

Yes. Windows shows this for new programs it has not seen many times yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

What happens after the 7 days?

The programs ask for a licence key. Your files stay on your computer and you can open them in any other program.

Does it run on an old computer?

Yes. Namma Office is made to start fast and stay smooth on older computers with Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and 4 GB of memory.

How do updates work?

Namma Office checks for a new version every few hours, downloads it quietly and installs it when you close the program. You can turn this off in the Updates window.

Something is not working. What do I do?

Choose Report a problem in the menu. It saves a report file to your Desktop and opens an email to us. Attach the file and press Send. The report never contains the text of your documents.

I bought a new computer.

Write to us with the new Machine ID and we will move your licence.
